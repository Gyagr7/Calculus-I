<title>Pre-Assignment: Pancake and the Case of the Broken GPS</title>

<p>
  Disaster at the Great Pasture Race! Pancake the alpaca's GPS collar
  glitched in the middle of her run. It stopped recording <em>where</em>
  she was. The only thing still working is her speedometer.
</p>

<p>
  Your job, detective: figure out where Pancake went, using nothing but
  her speed. About 10 minutes. Wrong answers are fine. Detectives guess
  all the time.
</p>

<p>
  <alert>Question 1.</alert> For the first 5 seconds, the speedometer
  reads a steady <m>4</m> meters per second. How many meters did Pancake
  cover?
</p>

<answer>
  <label>Distance in the first 5 seconds</label>
  <award>20</award>
</answer>

<p>
  Then Pancake speeds up. The speedometer now says her speed at time
  <m>t</m> is <m>v(t) = 2t</m> meters per second. Speed is changing, so
  you can't just multiply anymore. Time to interview some suspects.
</p>

<p>
  <alert>Question 2.</alert> Each suspect below claims to be Pancake's
  position function <m>s(t)</m>. Remember: velocity is the derivative of
  position. Differentiate each suspect. Which one could be telling the
  truth?
</p>

<answer>
  <label>Which suspect could be the position function?</label>
  <choice credit="1"><m>s(t) = t^2 + 7</m></choice>
  <choice credit="0"><m>s(t) = 2</m></choice>
  <choice credit="0"><m>s(t) = 2t^2</m></choice>
  <choice credit="0"><m>s(t) = t^2/2</m></choice>
</answer>

<p>
  Plot twist: <m>t^2</m>, <m>t^2 + 7</m>, and <m>t^2 - 5</m> all match
  the speedometer. Drag the slider and watch the whole family move. Every
  curve has the same steepness at every moment. They only disagree about
  where Pancake <em>started</em>.
</p>

<p>Shift the curve up or down: <slider name="C" from="-10" to="10" step="1" initialValue="0" /></p>

<graph xmin="-0.5" xmax="5" ymin="-12" ymax="36">
  <function>x^2 + $C</function>
</graph>

<p>
  So the speedometer alone can't crack the case. We need one more clue.
</p>

<p>
  <alert>Question 3.</alert> A witness saw Pancake at the barn door when
  the race began, <m>3</m> meters past the starting post. So
  <m>s(0) = 3</m>, and her speed is still <m>v(t) = 2t</m>. Where was
  Pancake, in meters past the starting post, at <m>t = 4</m> seconds?
</p>

<answer>
  <label>Position at t = 4</label>
  <award>19</award>
</answer>

<p>
  Case closed. Speed data gives you a whole family of possible paths.
  One clue about where she started picks out the real one. That is the
  whole idea behind antiderivatives and initial conditions.
</p>
